# Skin: Sci-Fi Apocalypse

Use for ruined cities, space survival, machine ethics, and high-stakes survival choices.

- Surface: blue-black with cold concrete gray; keep saturation for alarms and live signal only.
- Accent: electric cyan for verified systems, emergency red for a finite threat, acid green for contamination.
- Display: `Noto Serif SC` or clean sans depending on diegesis; never use terminal text as decoration.
- Motion: restrained scanning light or environmental drift, never a persistent HUD grid.
- Choice copy: make time, oxygen, trust, or route cost explicit.
- S1 framing: centered face against a believable cockpit, shelter, or ruined interior; no unreadable visor reflections.
