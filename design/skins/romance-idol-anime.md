# Skin: Romance Idol Anime

Use for stage-performance romance, idol routes, and emotionally reactive anime stories.

- Surface: near-black plum with saturated magenta only at the emotional peak.
- Accent: orchid for invitation and blush-pink for affection paths.
- Display: `ZCOOL XiaoWei`; long dialogue stays in `Noto Serif SC`.
- Motion: camera-light bloom and a soft scene crossfade. Do not add floating hearts or decorative stickers.
- Choice copy: frame the emotional intent, such as staying, encouraging, or asking the difficult question.
- S1 framing: 16:9, centered upper body, clear eyes, visible microphone or stage context only when story-relevant.
