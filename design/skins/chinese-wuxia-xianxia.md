# Skin: Chinese Wuxia Xianxia

Use for martial-arts quests, immortal cultivation, sect conflicts, and mythic romance.

- Surface: mountain ink, jade shadow, and deep midnight; avoid generic purple fantasy gradients.
- Accent: jade for balance, cinnabar for blood oath, moon-silver for spiritual revelation.
- Display: `Ma Shan Zheng` for concise scene titles only; `Noto Serif SC` for narrative and decisions.
- Motion: drifting mist, rain, sword-light, or falling leaves must belong to the current location.
- Choice copy: use action verbs such as protect, conceal, challenge, or leave.
- S1 framing: landscape 16:9, face and shoulders fully visible, robes and hair readable without blocking the eyes.
