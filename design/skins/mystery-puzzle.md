# Skin: Mystery Puzzle

Use for metaphysical cases, investigations, survival puzzles, and clue-led branches.

- Surface: black-indigo stage with fogged charcoal edges.
- Accent: muted amethyst for unstable clues; pale gold for confirmed truth.
- Display: `Noto Serif SC`; avoid playful handwritten type.
- Motion: a single slow focus shift when a clue is revealed; no busy particles.
- Choice copy: state the risk or revelation, not the system variable.
- S1 framing: face lit from a practical source such as a lantern, window, or ritual flame. Eyes must remain clear.
