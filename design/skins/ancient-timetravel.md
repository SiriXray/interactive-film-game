# Skin: Ancient Timetravel

Use for ancient-world survival, court stories, historical commerce, and modern knowledge crossing into the past.

- Surface: ink black with weathered blue, ember gold, or cinnabar selected by the chapter.
- Accent: subdued gold for agency; cinnabar only for danger, oath, or irreversible loss.
- Display: `Ma Shan Zheng` for short chapter titles; `Noto Serif SC` for all long interactive text.
- Motion: wind, firelight, rain, or lantern glow may anchor the scene. UI itself remains still and precise.
- Choice copy: use a concrete consequence and a personal reason; avoid abstract morality labels.
- S1 framing: 16:9 medium close-up, face centered and uncovered, era-consistent hair and costume, uncluttered practical background.
